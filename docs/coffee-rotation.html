Coffee rotation

The Coffee of the Day, the recipe cards, and the upcoming queue rotate by date. Drinks with both a Hot and an Iced build alternate Hot, Iced in menu order; Iced-only drinks are always Iced and skip the alternation. Each pass through the line flips the starting build, and 2026-10-06 is anchored on Biscoff.

Rotation contract

The rotation is the library in menu order, filtered to recipes with at least one Hot or Iced build. daysSinceEpoch counts local calendar days from the 2026-09-23 anchor and queueIndexForDate wraps that to a queue position, so the Coffee of the Day changes at local midnight.

turn      = number of both-build recipes before this one in the rotation
loop      = floor(elapsedDays / recipeCount)
single-build recipe        → its own build (Iced-only → Iced)
both builds: (loop + turn) even → Hot · odd → Iced

Six-drink example (3 and 5 are Iced-only)

First loop: Hot, Iced, Iced, Hot, Iced, Iced. After the reset: Iced, Hot, Iced, Iced, Iced, Hot.

Reset rule

Each loop flips the starting build. With an even number of both-build drinks, a loop that started Hot ends Iced and the next starts Iced; with an odd number the alternation continues across the reset.

Current menu, first loop

H I H I H I I H I I H I I H I for #01–#15; Iced-only drinks are #06, #10, #13.

Current menu, after the reset

I H I H I I H I H I I H I I H. The first loop ends Iced and the next begins Iced.

Dates: 2026-10-06 Biscoff (Hot), 2026-10-07 Matcha Caramel (Iced), 2026-10-08 Cheesecake (Iced, first of the new loop), 2026-10-09 Caramel (Hot), 2026-10-10 Sea Salt (Iced).

Iced-only drinks (Gula Melaka #06, Guava Spark Espresso #10, Calamansi Aerocano #13) are kept apart in src/data/recipes.ts, and a test fails if they become adjacent. An empty line has queue index 0 and falls back to Hot. No API, persistence, transaction, locking, idempotency, or rollback behavior is involved.

User-facing behavior

  • The featured panel, list cards and queue previews open on the scheduled build for their date and position.
  • Manual Hot/Iced changes remain available and last for the current view; the home-page control reads Schedule / Hot / Iced.
  • At local midnight, recipe, queue, position, date label and scheduled build update together.
  • Opening a recipe carries the active card or featured build into the modal; recipes with one available build use that build only.
