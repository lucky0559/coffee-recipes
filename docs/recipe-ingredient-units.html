A complete audit of the 13-recipe line, with Honey/Blue Agave corrected to the requested values and liquid-versus-dry units made consistent across recipe builds and notes.

Unit policy

Use ml for liquid or pourable ingredients

Water 40 ml, Milk 120 ml, Honey/Blue Agave 7 ml by default or 3 ml for Matcha Spiced, Caramel Syrup 15 ml, Spiced Biscuit Syrup 15 ml, Condensed Milk 15 ml, Patis 2 ml, whipping cream 30 ml, and cold-foam milk 15 ml.

Use g for dry numeric ingredients

Matcha Powder remains 4 g and Tibuok remains 0.3 g.

Retain operational measures where no conversion was supplied

Syrup pumps, espresso shots, one spoon of Biscoff Spread, a pinch of Sea Salt, and a splash of Ground Cinnamon remain as written. No numeric quantity was invented for these measures.
