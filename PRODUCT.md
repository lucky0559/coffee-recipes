# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Home coffee drinkers and small café teams who need a quick, reliable recipe reference while choosing a drink or preparing one. The primary job is to find a recipe, confirm its Hot or Iced ingredients, remember useful recipes, and share a recipe with someone else.

## Product Purpose

Brewline is a calm, visual coffee recipe library with a daily rotating recommendation. It should make a small recipe collection feel easy to browse and dependable to reference without requiring an account or network connection for core reading.

## Brand Personality

Warm, considered, editorial. Copy is concise and confident; the interface feels like a well-kept recipe notebook rather than a social feed or an operations dashboard.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS dashboards, noisy gamification, cartoon coffee clichés, decorative gradients, excessive glass effects, and interactions that hide essential recipe information behind unnecessary steps.

## Design Principles

- Keep the recipe visible: discovery controls should reduce the collection without obscuring ingredients.
- Respect the ritual: motion and feedback should be quiet, brief, and useful.
- Prefer local-first reliability: reading, favorites, history, and shared links should degrade gracefully offline.
- Use explicit content: labels, allergen notes, substitutions, and temperature state must be understandable without color alone.
- Make the small collection feel intentional: every added feature should strengthen browsing, remembering, or sharing.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Support keyboard-only navigation, visible focus, reduced motion, screen-reader labels, semantic controls, strong contrast, and touch targets that remain comfortable on small screens. Avoid treating dietary substitutions as guarantees; allergen copy should identify known recipe ingredients and encourage users to verify packaged products.
